SCP: Containment Escape Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SCP: Containment Escape ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android game (package com.cukkacreatives.scp.containment.escape).

1. Information We Collect

The game does not require account registration and does not ask for your name, email address, or payment details. It does not include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchase services, and we do not receive your gameplay data.

Local gameplay data: preferences, progress, and other in-game state are stored on your device so that you can continue playing.

2. How We Use Information

Local gameplay data is used only by the game on your device, to remember your settings and progress. If you email us, we use what you send to respond to you.

3. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google Play and related Google services may process install, device, purchase, review, security, and diagnostic information under Google's own policies. Review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell or rent personal information, and the game does not send your gameplay data to us or to other parties. We may disclose correspondence or other information we hold if required by law or necessary to protect users, the app, or our legal rights.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device and is generally removed when you uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and restore settings. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You can clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove locally stored data, and you can contact us using the details below regarding applicable privacy rights.

7. Data Deletion Requests

The game does not create user accounts. Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup settings. To request deletion of personal data that we control, such as support correspondence, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "SCP: Containment Escape Data Deletion Request." We will respond within a reasonable period and delete applicable data unless retention is legally required. Data controlled by Google can be managed through your Google Play Store, Google Account, or Android privacy settings.

8. Children's Privacy

SCP: Containment Escape is a horror game and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information we hold. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If a future version of the game adds advertising, analytics, purchases, or other services that process data, this page will be updated before that version is released. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

11. This Website

These static pages do not include advertising SDKs, analytics scripts, account registration, or tracking cookies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, to serve and secure the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Opening Google Play or another external link is subject to that service's own privacy policy. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include so that we can respond.
